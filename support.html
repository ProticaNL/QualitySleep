Getting Started

How does QualitySleep work?

QualitySleep monitors your sleep sounds through your iPhone and detects snoring. When snoring is detected, your paired Apple Watch gently vibrates to help interrupt snoring without a loud alarm.

Do I need an Apple Watch?

Yes. QualitySleep requires an Apple Watch paired with your iPhone to provide vibration feedback when snoring is detected.

How do I start monitoring?

Wear your Apple Watch, open QualitySleep on your iPhone, and start a recording session before going to sleep. Stop the session when you wake up.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why isn't my Apple Watch vibrating?

Make sure your Apple Watch is connected to your iPhone and that haptic feedback is enabled in your Apple Watch settings. Also check that QualitySleep is actively monitoring for snoring.

Can I listen to my snoring recordings?

Yes. QualitySleep saves short recordings of detected snoring sounds. You can review your previous sleep sessions and listen to individual recordings directly in the app.

Will the vibrations completely stop my snoring?

Not necessarily. Gentle vibrations may encourage you to change your sleeping position or briefly wake up, potentially interrupting snoring. Results vary from person to person.

Is QualitySleep a medical device?

No. QualitySleep is designed for personal sleep monitoring and awareness. It is not intended to diagnose, treat, or prevent medical conditions such as sleep apnea. If you suspect a sleep disorder, consult a healthcare professional.

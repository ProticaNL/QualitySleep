Privacy Policy

How QualitySleep handles sleep recordings, snoring detection, and Apple Watch vibration alerts.

Effective date: November 5, 2026
Privacy at a glance

QualitySleep analyzes snoring sounds on your iPhone and uses your paired Apple Watch for vibration feedback. We do not upload your audio recordings to our servers. Recordings and sleep session information are stored locally on your device, subject to your device's backup settings.

1. Who operates QualitySleep?

QualitySleep ("the App") is provided by Protica ("we", "us", "our"). For privacy questions, contact us at qualitysleep@protica.nl.

2. What information does the App process?

Microphone audio

When you choose to start a monitoring session and permit microphone access, the App processes sound from your iPhone's microphone to detect possible snoring. Sounds in your surroundings, including other people's voices, may be captured when monitoring is active.

Snoring recordings and session details

The App may save short audio clips associated with detected snoring, along with information such as session dates, start and end times, recording durations, and detection markers. This lets you review and play back recordings.

Apple Watch feedback

When the App detects snoring, it can send a detection signal to your paired Apple Watch to request a gentle vibration. The App does not need to transfer recorded audio to your watch to request this feedback.

Support requests

If you email us for support, we receive the information you choose to share, such as your email address, the contents of your message, and any files you attach.

3. How is information used?

QualitySleep uses the information described above solely to provide its core functions:

  • Monitoring microphone audio during user-started sessions.
  • Detecting possible snoring and displaying detection indicators.
  • Saving and playing back short snoring recordings and session history.
  • Triggering vibration feedback on a paired Apple Watch.
  • Responding to support and privacy inquiries.

QualitySleep does not use these recordings to display personalized advertising or sell your sleep information.

4. Storage, sharing, and third parties

Snore detection is performed locally on your iPhone. QualitySleep does not upload your microphone recordings, detected audio clips, or sleep session history to developer-operated servers.

We do not sell or share your recordings with advertising networks or data brokers. The App uses Apple platform functionality to communicate with your paired Apple Watch. Depending on your iPhone settings, locally stored app data may be included in backups handled by Apple. Such backups are governed by your Apple settings and Apple's terms and privacy practices.

If you contact support, your email provider and our email service provider may process the correspondence as needed to transmit and respond to your message. Service providers handling personal information on our behalf are expected to apply appropriate data protection safeguards.

5. Your choices and microphone permission

Microphone access is needed for snoring detection. You control when monitoring begins and ends. You can stop a monitoring session at any time and revoke microphone permission through your iPhone's Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone.

Revoking microphone permission prevents further microphone-based monitoring until permission is granted again. It does not automatically delete recordings already stored on your device.

Please make sure people nearby are aware of any audio recording where required by applicable law.

6. How long is information kept, and how can it be deleted?

Locally saved recordings and session details can remain on your iPhone until you remove them. You can remove the App's local data by deleting QualitySleep from your iPhone. If your app version offers individual recording or session deletion, you can use those controls as well.

Deleting the App does not necessarily remove copies included in backups managed through your Apple account. You can manage device backups through your Apple settings.

We retain support correspondence only as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your request, resolve related issues, or comply with applicable legal obligations. You may ask us to delete support information by contacting us.

7. Legal basis and your rights

Where the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) applies, microphone monitoring is initiated by your choice and with your device permission. Where consent is required for processing, including any applicable sensitive or health-related data processing, it can be withdrawn by stopping monitoring and revoking microphone access. For support correspondence, we rely on our legitimate interest in handling requests or on steps taken at your request, as appropriate. We may also process information where required by law.

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to request access to your personal data, correction, deletion, restriction of processing, data portability, or to object to processing. You may also withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. To exercise these rights for information we control, contact us using the email address below.

If you are in the European Economic Area, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority, including the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).

8. Security

QualitySleep relies on iOS app storage and device security controls for locally stored information. You should keep your iPhone updated and use a device passcode. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

9. This website

This support and privacy website is hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical information, including visitor IP addresses, for security and operation of its hosting service. For details, see GitHub's Privacy Statement.

We do not add our own advertising trackers or analytics scripts to this website.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the App's functionality or our data practices change. We will publish the updated version here and revise its effective date. If a change requires additional consent, we will request it when required by law.
